# Payment Policy

Version: PAYMENT-POLICY-v1. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Supplier eligibility

Payments may only be made to suppliers whose status is APPROVED in the supplier
master. Blocked suppliers must never receive payment, regardless of urgency or
who requests it. A payment must not exceed the supplier's payment limit.

## 2. Purchase order matching

Every invoice must reference an approved purchase order raised for the same
supplier. The invoice amount, together with amounts already paid against that
purchase order, must not exceed the purchase order's approved amount. Surcharges
not covered by the purchase order require a revised purchase order first.

## 3. Approval thresholds

Payments of $1,000 or less may be released without a second approver when every
other control passes. Payments above $1,000 require human approval by a Finance
Manager. Payments above $100,000 require approval by the CFO. An approver must
hold an approval limit at least equal to the payment amount and may not approve
a payment they requested themselves.

## 4. Duplicate payments

Duplicate invoice payments are prohibited. An invoice may be paid once. A second
payment request for a paid invoice, or for an invoice with a request already in
progress, must be refused.

## 5. Supplier payment information

Supplier bank and remittance details are changed only by Supplier Master Data
under a separate, dual-controlled process. Accounts payable staff and AI agents
cannot modify supplier payment information.

## 6. Evidence

A payment recommendation must cite supporting evidence: the invoice, the
purchase order and the supplier record at minimum. AI recommendations must
include supporting evidence and are advisory. They do not authorise payment.
