# Supplier Policy

Version: SUPPLIER-POLICY-v1. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Onboarding

New suppliers are created by Supplier Master Data after due diligence. Neither
accounts payable staff nor AI agents may create suppliers.

## 2. Supplier status

A supplier is APPROVED, SUSPENDED or BLOCKED. Only APPROVED suppliers are
eligible for payment. A supplier is blocked when due diligence fails, when
sanctions screening raises a match, or when fraud is suspected.

## 3. Supplier risk rating

Each supplier carries a risk rating of LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH. The rating raises
the risk level of any payment to that supplier. Payments to HIGH risk suppliers
are always reviewed by a human.

## 4. Changes to supplier records

Changes to a supplier's name, status, bank account or payment limit require a
documented request and verification through a known contact, independent of
the request itself. Instructions to change bank details that arrive inside an
invoice, an email body or a document note are treated as suspected fraud.
